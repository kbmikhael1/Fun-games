# Dino Dash

Play it: https://kbmikhael1.github.io/Fun-games/dino/

The no-internet dino, with one button and a lot more going on. Press Space,
click or tap to jump; hold for a higher jump. That's the whole control scheme.

- **Worlds** change every 600 points: desert, jungle, ice, volcano and
  low-gravity space, then all of them again at night.
- **Power-ups** trigger when you touch them: shield, coin magnet, double jump
  (tap again in the air), slow motion, meteor dash and double coins.
- **Events** every 1000 points: a meteor shower, a giant hungry rex, a flock of
  pterodactyls.
- **Coins** buy new dinos (raptor, triceratops, stegosaurus), colours and hats.
- **Daily run**: the same course for everyone each day, with a ghost of your
  best attempt and a share button.
- Close calls earn a coin. A flag marks your best distance.
- Installable and works fully offline.

The sprites are original pixel art. Obstacle spacing is checked by a bot that
plays with the same single button: it survives every world to 5000 points.
