How to be a god

Watch

Every creature has genes for size, speed, diet, fur, intelligence and more. They eat, flee, hunt, mate and die. Children inherit a mix of their parents' genes plus small mutations. When a group drifts far enough from its ancestors it becomes a new species.

Look closer

Scroll to zoom, drag to pan. Click any creature to see its genes, family and thoughts. The side panel holds the list of species, the Chronicle, the Tree of Life and the census.

Intervene

Choose a power from the left: Create, Destroy, Bless, Speak. Most powers are aimed with a click on the world.

Walk among them

Incarnate as any creature and live its life. When you die you can be reborn as one of your children and guide a bloodline.

The spark

A clever, numerous species becomes intelligent: it forms tribes, builds camps, farms, and towns. Perform miracles near them and they may build you a shrine.

Undo history

Rewind to any recent year and try a different path.
