How to be a god

Your people

Every person has a name, a family, a job and a memory. They gather food, build homes, marry, have children and grow old. Click anyone to meet them.

They pray to you

When they are hungry, sick, scared or grieving, they pray. Their prayers appear on the left. Answer them and they love you. Ignore them and they doubt you.

Loved or feared

Every miracle and every disaster is remembered. A loving god gets festivals and white temples. A feared god gets offerings, obedience and black cathedrals.

From caves to a city

Speed up time (top) and watch them discover farming, bronze, iron, castles, factories and finally skyscrapers. Some day they may reach the stars.

Powers

Nature, Wrath, Grace and Hand. Rain, lightning, meteors, tornadoes, plagues, healing, blessings, raising the dead. Pick one at the bottom, then click the world.

Walk among them

Choose a person and press Become human. Walk with W A S D, talk, chop, fight and preach. When your body dies, you return to the sky.
